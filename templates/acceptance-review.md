## 必查 / Checks

- [ ] 产物存在且能打开 / Artifacts exist and can be opened.
- [ ] 核心事实与来源一致 / Material claims match their sources.
- [ ] 计算或测试有可核查输出 / Calculations or tests have inspectable output.
- [ ] 未授权动作、漏项与重复操作已检查 / Unauthorized actions, omissions, and duplicates checked.
- [ ] 未知和缺失信息已明确列出 / Unknowns and missing information disclosed.
- [ ] 没有用语气、自评分或篇幅代替质量判断 / Confidence, self-scores, and length not used as proof.

## 判定 / Decision

- 结论 / Accept, reject, or blocked:
- 必须修正的事项 / Required corrections:
- 检查无法覆盖的风险 / Limitations of this review:
- 是否需要人复核 / Human review needed:
- 漏检与误报的后续记录 / Subsequent false negatives and false positives:

未知的硬约束不能视作通过。另一个模型担任验收者，也可能与执行者共享盲点。
An unknown hard gate is not a pass. A model reviewer may share the executor's blind spots.
